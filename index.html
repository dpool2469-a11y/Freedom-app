LUMP-SUM REPAYMENT ORDER

Which loan should I repay first?

Usually, after keeping an adequate emergency reserve and checking prepayment charges, paying extra toward the highest effective interest rate saves the most interest (avalanche method). Smallest-balance-first can provide motivation (snowball method). Compare tax benefits, secured-loan risks and lender terms before deciding.
